Run & Bun snapshot decoder

Select your ROM and a John GBA .jg0 through .jg9 snapshot. Everything is decoded locally; files are not uploaded. Battery .sav files and .js screenshot previews are not supported here.

The JSON includes your party, boxes, bag and partially decoded battle memory. It is not yet a simulator-ready battle or a legality check. Battle activity and phase remain unknown.


  
Download the JSON, open the calculator, then choose Import box.
    Import replaces all saved collections. Current HP, PP, inventory and battle details are retained in
    gameStateSnapshot; the calculator currently uses only the conventional sets and party.
